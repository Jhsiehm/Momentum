# Multi-Congress point-in-time expansion — design v1

Dated November 5, 2026. **Proposed design, scoring disabled.** This is a data-acquisition and evaluation specification, not a completed expansion or an executable permission to fit. Commit this design before acquisition/eligibility review and register an immutable executable protocol, exact manifests, code/dependency hashes and human gate receipts before any future scoring. No candidate selection, calibration, new predictions, or scores were produced for this design.

The accompanying [audit](../operations/runs/20261105-historical-data-audit.md) supplies actual local counts and identifies exposed studies. Preserve every existing experiment, its source bytes, forecasts, protocol, code snapshot and negative result. Amendments must be new artifacts referencing parent hashes, never in-place repairs of frozen studies.

## Population and target

Acquire full H.R./S. inventories for Congresses **113–117**, both chambers, including missing 113, 115 and 117 inventories. Reuse verified raw bytes where possible, but build a new manifest. Acquire all selected identities in deterministic Congress/chamber/numeric-bill order; no random subsampling, positive enrichment, replacement, or stopping once a favorable number of enactments is reached. The complete 114/116 saved directories currently contain 10,074/14,148 IDs. Counts for the unacquired Congresses are unknown. Directory completeness is not proof that every introduced bill is present: reconcile identity sets against an independent official listing; retain discrepancies and unavailable identities in the denominator.

Target: own-bill **public-law enactment before the end of its Congress**, not enactment at any later date, passage, importance, or provisions incorporated into other vehicles. Use the existing `congress_end` UTC endpoint and date-precision conventions. One checkpoint per bill, verified introduction + **180 days**, fixed before expansion. Exclude checkpoints at/after Congress end and bills already enacted by the checkpoint; same-day outcome/checkpoint or session-end ambiguity requires adjudication, not a favorable assumption. Report these exclusions by Congress/chamber and outcome where known. This is a conditional risk set of bills still unenacted at age 180; do not generalize to all introduced bills.

The 114th import contains 179 recorded enactments satisfying only the timing screen, versus 326 recorded eventual positives. That is an acquisition opportunity, not 179 certified eligible positive families. Full inventories across five Congresses are proposed to retain natural positives without distorting prevalence. **Sufficient audited positives are a release gate, not a promised result.**

5. Construct a complete law membership census per Congress, including joint resolutions for census reconciliation even though predictions cover H.R./S. ## Family separation is a hard preflight gate

Create a versioned graph over the **entire acquisition universe**, not separately over each split. Include companion, reintroduction, related-bill and adjudicated substantial shared-provision links, with cited evidence and adjudication status. Include out-of-sample intermediary nodes so transitive overlaps cannot escape detection. Identical-title matching is a screening heuristic; generic titles can overmerge and renamed reintroductions can escape. Unreviewed singletons do not establish independence.

No family component may cross training, validation or protected evaluation. Resolve cross-boundary components by assigning ownership to the latest partition and **purging earlier-partition members**, never moving future rows into training. Retain every purge in the ledger. For train/validation, take one representative per training family using earliest eligible checkpoint then canonical bill ID, chosen without labels. Retain all evaluation bills but report distinct positive families and cluster uncertainty by family. Family knowledge learned later may be used solely as a conservative purge, never as an historical feature.

Require a preflight assertion over adjudicated connected-component IDs: intersections of all partition family sets are empty; no duplicate identity; all training Congresses precede validation/evaluation; all features and training labels pass their origin-time checks. Missing family adjudication, unresolved cross-links or mismatch between metadata/event/resolution family IDs blocks scoring. Existing runtime family-ID equality checks are insufficient for this stronger gate. This design does not pretend the new full-graph gate is already integrated into existing scoring commands.

For future evaluation, lock the model only after an independent custodian purges known development-family overlap. Newly discovered cross-family links after prediction must be reported as protocol violations; do not silently delete unfavorable cases or refit the locked model. Publish prespecified contaminated-family sensitivity results separately and withhold the primary independence claim when separation fails.

## Positive-case and precision gates

Before any future fitting, require at least **200 distinct eligible positive training families**, both classes, and at least **100 eligible positive validation families** after all provenance and family purges. For protected evaluation require **100 positive families per Congress and 200 pooled**. These are design minima, not a power calculation or proof of predictive sufficiency. At 100 independent positives the worst-case binomial standard error for sensitivity is about 0.05 (roughly ±0.10 at 95%); family dependence and only two Congress clusters worsen uncertainty. A paired Brier improvement needs its own prespecified precision analysis using development-only variance, not protected outcomes.

An independent custodian may report only whether the protected sample-size gate passes, after the full fixed window closes. Do not repeatedly inspect positives, alter age, extend the horizon, change the target, enrich positives, or replace missing cases until it passes. If a full Congress is insufficient, report underpowered/blocked and register additional **future whole Congresses** before observing their outcomes. The full-census design preserves the denominator and prevalence; it cannot guarantee that provenance and family review will leave enough positives.

## Prespecified evaluation, not executed

Baseline: Beta(1,1) smoothed enactment rate `(positive_training_families + 1)/(training_families + 2)`, on the exact same eligible/purged training risk set as the candidate. Candidate reference is the unchanged six publication-proxy features and fixed structured logistic specification in the current publication protocol; do not import the apparent stronger-regularization winner from exposed comparisons as a validated choice. Any additional candidates need separate registration before development scoring.

Primary: paired candidate-minus-baseline Brier loss, per Congress and pooled with equal Congress weights. Secondary: log loss, precision-recall summary with prevalence reference, fixed-bin reliability with counts, and coverage/exclusion rates. Do not report sensitivity without a threshold prespecified on development data. Count both bills and positive families. Report all planned comparisons, including negative results and failures. Any model selection/calibration belongs solely in registered development data; no test-driven reselection.

Report family-clustered intervals and per-Congress estimates. Two protected Congresses are too few for dependable Congress-clustered inference; disclose that limitation and avoid population-wide significance claims. Conditional family bootstraps omit training variability; any refit/bootstrap uncertainty plan must be fixed on development data first. Multiplicity, missing predictions, source failures and family contamination need prespecified reporting, not post-hoc filtering. No deployment, promotion or validated-improvement claim follows automatically from passing data gates or obtaining a favorable point estimate.

## Acceptance sequence and blockers

1. Register acquisition manifest and this design's commit/hash; acquire complete inventories and bytes under a new run identity.
2. Publish a no-score flow table: inventory → acquired → verified introduction → before-end checkpoint → unenacted at checkpoint → historical features → dated/complete labels → family purge → human review → eligible, with positives and unknowns at every step.
3. Audit every positive and every uncertain/mismatched record; perform a prespecified outcome-blind sample of remaining records. Preserve the existing designated 25 human reviews separately. No automated pass substitutes for human receipts.
4. Rebuild offline, verify hashes, resolve source/extraction discrepancies, satisfy family/time gates and positive minima; register the executable scoring protocol and access controls.
5. Only then fit/score according to the separately authorized protocol. Fail closed and publish a blocked report if a gate fails.

Current blockers: missing full 113/115/117 inventories and broad 116 import; 10,057 unresolved local outcomes; no main-store metadata demonstrably available at age180; incomplete historical rendition identity and action histories; all 10,392 local families unadjudicated; no expanded eligibility ledger; insufficient audited positives; active independent acquisition lease; and no protected-label custodian.
