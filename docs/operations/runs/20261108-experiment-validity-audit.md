# Experiment validity audit and bounded corrective rerun

November 8, 2026. **Exploratory design diagnostics only.** Reproducible software defects were addressed in separate versions. The audit does not certify every edge case, legal accuracy, historical eligibility or predictive improvement.

The rerun reduced raw locators from **2,727 to 2,717** and reference-quotation intersections from **449/501 to 447/501**. Eleven hit identities were removed and one narrower identity added; two intersections were lost and none recovered. These negative results are preserved without post-result tuning. Five unvalidated legacy suggestions were quarantined. No accepted labels, model fits or predictive tests were produced.

## Question, population and exposure

Can leaf-local expansion, a narrower negative-subject rule, affirmative amendment provenance and strict source checks address reproduced defects while exposing all coverage/workload changes? This is a software repair and fixed-design diagnostic, not a semantic quality or prediction experiment. Three agent collaborators audited code, source integrity and methods and cross-reviewed fixes. They are not independent external validators or qualified human certification.

Inputs: the same forty design bills, eight each from Congresses 113–117, 337,823 reading-view characters, twelve mechanisms, 480 statuses and 501 provisional corrected reference effects. These are the existing v2-rubric subset of the original fifty design cases; the other ten are not silently pooled. Corrected reference v3 remains frozen. The fifty quality cases were not opened. No acquisition, new outcomes, quality sampling, model training, forecasts, promotion or schedule changes occurred. The source texts, prior reviews and synthetic counterexamples informed these rules; no independent generalization claim follows.

## Decision and next work

**Do not promote this extractor as validated.** Keep the integrity guard and universal quarantine in new workflows; treat v4/filterv2 as a frozen design candidate with documented regressions. Never overwrite its negative result to obtain a favorable count.

The next separately registered engineering study must test longer and coordinated prohibition subjects, noun-phrase attachments and qualified amendment prefixes using synthetic contrasts first. Preserve both the successful no-cost/cross-block fixes and counterexamples where narrow syntax loses valid material. Include full tables/XML structure and broader reference-completeness review in readiness work. Exact next jobs and pass/block decisions are in the recovery plan.

Then complete source/version/family and reference prerequisites before opening the original protected quality set. Preserve the original two-qualified-reviewer plus adjudicator requirement; AI consensus is not a replacement certification. No predictor trial until historical availability, class/family/calibration and temporal-isolation gates pass. Train/calibrate on eligible earlier data only; compare all arms on identical populations. Self-updating remains a separately frozen policy using matured labels and immutable forecasts.

For the 60% ambition, specify `1 − Brier_model/Brier_rate ≥0.60` against an earlier-trained rate baseline, and preregister the `Brier_model −0.40×Brier_rate` contrast with joint uncertainty/multiplicity controls before protected outcomes. Retain existing practical-effect and prospective gates. No current result demonstrates 60% predictive improvement.
