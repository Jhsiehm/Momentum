2026-11-08. Exposed design development only; no predictive or semantic accuracy claim.

**Outcome:** recovered five useful prohibition locators and two reference-quotation overlaps lost in the previous v4 run, without removing any v4 hit. Restored two struck-operand flags. ## Question and fixed design

Can five bounded subject patterns and qualified structural amendment-prefix handling recover the observed v4/filter-v2 regressions while preserving previous boundary/negation counterexamples? This is an additional explicitly exposed design iteration, not independent validation. The previous negative run remains frozen. Parser coverage, identity/time-edge fixtures and independent source/reference readiness remain outstanding next stages.

Population: the same 40 bills,8 per Congress 113–117, 337,823 reading-view characters, 12 mechanism fields and 501 provisional reference effects. They are the existing 40-case subset of the original 50 design cases. The remaining 10 design cases are not silently pooled; all 50 quality cases remain unopened. Reference v3, raw source views and case order remain fixed. No new sources, outcomes, labels, model fitting or protected evaluation.

## Configuration and registration

The v5 wrapper preserves all v4 hit identities and quarantined suggestions. It adds five generic subject families, without bill IDs or reference offsets encoded in rules: franchise authority; information with bounded modifiers; derived evidence; fixed rate of pay; and explicitly coordinated roads/routes. New matches stay inside complete leaf text nodes. Conjunction/sentence bridges, no-cost/no-limit phrases and negative modals are challenged by synthetic contrasts. The underlying frozen legacy hits retain their known semantic risks.

Filter v3 only restores a previously removed deletion reason when a qualified scope prefix has a complete chain to an affirmative amendment declaration. It permits only bounded subdivision references, including “as redesignated,” with limits of 512 characters per scope/lead-in, 4 comma-separated scopes and 12 ancestor levels. Negated/narrative/insertion/quoted-boundary contrasts remain. This is syntax-based review triage, not legal interpretation. Previous and legacy decisions/evidence are retained.

Registration was written before new 40-case extraction. It pins 124 input files and 31 code/test files, Python version, all 33 base expansion rules plus 5 supplemental rules, filter rules, baseline/reference identities, metric, exposure and stopping policy. Source/reference guards, exact legacy preservation, v4 identity preservation and quarantine checks run per bill. Extraction/triage repeat per bill to check determinism and nonmutation, and all registered input/code hashes are rechecked at the end. No post-result rule changes. One fixed 40-case run; no replacement or new review sampling.

## Metrics and aggregate results

A hit is unique by mechanism/start/end within a bill. Coordinates are zero-based half-open reading-view characters. An effect quote is covered when at least one same-mechanism hit intersects it; multiple hits count the effect once. Separate effects sharing a broad quote remain separate units. Retained coverage requires an unflagged hit. Coverage is not semantic recall; flags are not legal judgments. No population accuracy estimate or confidence interval is inferred from these repeatedly inspected design bills.
