# BICAM context acceptance and next comparison, version 1

November 8, 2026. A source-acceptance decision document, not an experiment registration. No model fitting, new scores, bill-content review, outcome inspection or source eligibility certification was performed for this document. This work inspected public source/schema documentation and existing Momentum research reports. The separately recorded inventory audit owns empirical archive counts and sample results; this document does not infer them from advertised coverage.

## Decision

Expand historical identities and audit joined sources before another fit. Prioritize a compact dated political/procedural context block over another text representation search. Retain the stage-rate reference that outperformed the recent text diagnostic; retain full introduced bill language as a separately evaluated information source. Do not tune on the 595 exposed evaluation cases. Neither larger downloads nor Docker establish historical availability.

Keep the target unchanged: own-bill public-law enactment after the day-180 checkpoint and before Congress end. Apply the existing protocol's cutoff/date-precision, matured-label, negative-coverage, family and review rules. “Passed” is not a substitute for enactment; provision incorporation and committee survival are different targets. The eight unresolved rendition cases remain unresolved unless new evidence actually resolves them.

## What the source documentation establishes

BICAM describes a downloader for joined congressional materials from Congress.gov and GovInfo and demonstrates linking sponsors to members by Bioguide identifier. Its software documentation is not a point-in-time certification or proof that every archive table matches its examples. [Author repository](https://github.com/bicam-data/bicam).

The prior bounded check established bill, text and action headers and an archive directory containing sponsor, cosponsor and action-committee tables. It did not inspect those latter tables' columns or contents. Those names are acquisition leads, not verified field schemas. Text fields' body-versus-URL semantics and introduced-version join completeness require the separate corpus audit. [Prior source report](reviews/20261108-historical-dataset-options.md).

GovInfo distinguishes IH and IS as introduced House and Senate versions. Later engrossed/enrolled editions represent different legislative stages. Selecting a named introduced version establishes edition semantics, not the availability of today's bytes at a historical cutoff. [Official version definitions](https://www.govinfo.gov/help/bills).

Congress.gov distinguishes legislative event dates from record update dates; cosponsor histories include joining/withdrawal dates. Its current aggregate counts cannot reconstruct a prior snapshot by themselves. [Official bill endpoint documentation](https://github.com/LibraryOfCongress/api.congress.gov/blob/main/Documentation/BillEndpoint.md).

The member endpoint supplies service terms and current affiliation; its current-party description explicitly cautions about party changes. Year-level terms cannot resolve a within-year switch without more evidence. [Official member endpoint documentation](https://github.com/LibraryOfCongress/api.congress.gov/blob/main/Documentation/MemberEndpoint.md).

A further acquisition lead is the [author deposit at Harvard Dataverse](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/TALQZL). Indexed primary-source discovery encountered by the coordinating agent describes version 4, an MIT license, scraped HTML in `raw_text`, and a smaller deposit variant omitting that field. This reviewer could not independently retrieve the live deposit metadata: the DOI redirect was inaccessible, the landing page required JavaScript verification, and the API route was unavailable through the browser tool. Consequently these are discovery leads only: neither the archive-wide license verdict nor measured text semantics is upgraded. Verify the exact deposit version, variant, file manifest, license and checksums before choosing it; do not download or substitute it silently. A license attached to one deposit would not automatically license a differently distributed website variant.

## Three distinct acceptance statuses

Each field record must carry bill identity, value, source URL/member, source hash and locator, effective/event time plus precision, publication/availability evidence plus precision, capture time, transformation version and disposition reason. Preserve disagreement rather than filling it from later evidence.

- **Historically eligible:** identity and semantics verified; the value was effective and demonstrably public by the checkpoint under the governing source/date rules; exact source binding and review requirements satisfied. Event date alone is insufficient. A later capture may corroborate an earlier source only with the required independent historical evidence.
- **Retrospectively reconstructible only:** a current source reports a dated earlier fact, but contemporaneous availability or rendition history is unresolved. It may support a separately declared retrospective study after its own registration, never an automatic pass of the forecasting gates.
- **Unavailable or conflicted:** missing, ambiguous, inconsistent or unsupported. Preserve null/reason; do not replace it with zero, final status, present affiliation or a later edition.

These statuses are per field and per case; accepting one field does not certify an entire bill. Source `updated_at`, ZIP Last-Modified and today's retrieval time are not first-publication timestamps. Date-only values follow the existing conservative date-precision policy; do not invent midnight timestamps or infer within-day order.

## Introduced-text join acceptance

1. Enumerate actual introduced H.R./S. identities in declared completed Congresses, independent of outcomes and source availability. Validate Congress, bill type and number against canonical identity. Reserved numbers without genuine introduction do not become negative examples. Count exclusions and compare with an official inventory before calling coverage complete.
2. Join the text member on canonical bill identity and an explicitly verified edition mapping: H.R. to IH; S. to IS. Audit the archive's `type` values and version reference rather than assuming it already uses those codes. Reject cross-Congress and wrong-chamber joins.
3. Require one unambiguous introduced source per bill. Identical duplicate hashes may collapse with all source rows retained in an audit; conflicting introduced records remain conflicts. Never choose the latest, longest or most complete-looking record after seeing outcomes. A revised printing needs its own dated version decision.
4. Verify that text is actual content or resolve a verified official URL under a separately bounded collection plan. Check fetched identity and edition against both metadata and document content; retain bytes, hashes and acquisition failures. XML, PDF and HTML are different renditions, not interchangeable historical evidence.
5. Verify document date and introduction chronology against the checkpoint. Preserve full bill language with the already frozen structural transformations when the format supports them. Unsupported format or ambiguous structure remains an explicit exclusion; do not silently substitute summaries/final text.
6. Assign historical or retrospective status using the availability evidence above. Group duplicates, companions and reintroductions under the existing family rules before cohort separation. The original human/source/family gates are unchanged.

## Smallest next predictive comparison, conditional on readiness

Select this question before new scores: **does a compact, dated context block improve the six-feature anchor on a broader matched historical population, while also beating the fixed stage-rate reference?** The proposed initial block is sponsor affiliation at introduction, active cosponsor count at cutoff, referral breadth and elapsed time since the last qualifying action. Include the block only if all four have audited semantics and a predeclared missing-data policy; otherwise record the failed readiness decision and issue a separately reviewed design before any fitting. Do not pick whichever subset looks best on outcomes. Majority alignment, party mix, effectiveness, schedules and new text architectures are deferred.

This is a recommendation, not a released fit. The future registration must fix exact sources, counts, period membership, feature encodings, missingness policy, class/family sufficiency, optimizer/calibration choices, fit budget, score access and stopping before execution. No ad hoc minimum-count reinterpretation is authorized here. Use the same target, populations and source exclusions for the two arms; fit preprocessing on earlier training only. Retain matched historical-rate and stage-rate references. Evaluate raw Brier and log loss, incremental loss against the anchor, skill against both references, class-specific losses, calibration bins and family-aware uncertainty with exact denominators. Any calibration must use a separate earlier partition with the governing coverage requirements.

The recently scored 595 cases remain exposed development data, not the new confirmatory test. Choose later untouched evaluation identities and family separation using source readiness and an exposure registry, before opening outcomes. Previously inspected labels or aggregate results still count as exposure. If no untouched, sufficiently supported population exists, declare a new study retrospective exploratory before fitting or wait for prospective outcomes; do not relabel the old cohort as validation. New source acquisition alone does not make an exposed period untouched.

The next data work is bounded inventory, then introduced-source and dated-context join auditing with every missing/conflicted case retained. It is not another unbounded source search and it is not another fit on the same text comparison. Mature historical outcomes provide supervised labels; automatic retraining/promotion remains a later, separately governed process. This document neither establishes a 60% Brier reduction nor certifies any new historical forecast.
