November 8, 2026. Status: **new engineering and research-readiness plan; no amendment to frozen protocols, quality release or predictive study authorization**. This document consolidates the [methods audit](reviews/20261108-experiment-validity-methods.md), its thirteen sealed findings, and new adversarial software repairs. The immediate goal is a defensible assisted-annotation workflow and a credible next experiment. Success includes honest negative, inconclusive and blocked outcomes.

## Semantic evaluation and statistical requirements

The [original E0 plan](BILL_CONTEXT_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_V1.md) controls quality: two qualified reviewers independently annotate every full quality document without candidate output or each other's work; a third adjudicates. Preserve originals, uncertainty and omissions. Require every retained evidence reference to resolve, zero unresolved identity/future-availability errors, and at least twenty positive quality bills for a mechanism before applying precision ≥95% and recall ≥90%. Sparse categories cannot pass on a perfect zero/small denominator. The [detailed extractor draft](MECHANISM_EXTRACTOR_EVALUATION_DRAFT_V1.md) proposes additional joint effect thresholds; settle these prospectively, not after quality exposure. Qualification of an assisted workflow does not qualify autonomous extraction.

For semantic scoring, require a source-bound exact population and all twelve statuses, complete effect inventories, frozen granularity and externally adjudicated compatibility graph. Maximum-cardinality one-to-one matching prevents broad predictions from absorbing multiple reference effects; duplicate predictions remain false positives and unmatched reference effects false negatives. Report joint effect precision/recall, mechanism/operation cells, condition errors, abstention, unsupported certainty on uncertain references, bill-level exact-set recovery, micro totals and equal-bill macro summaries. Use bill/family-aware descriptive uncertainty and expose sparse cells; effects and hits within a bill are not independent observations. The guard authenticates supplied structure/content only after its caller verifies registered envelopes; it does not provide historical or semantic certification.

For prediction, preserve [PAPER_PROTOCOL_V1](PAPER_PROTOCOL_V1.md) and [the methods clarification](BILL_CONTEXT_METHODS_CLARIFICATION_V1.md). Define the population scope of 200 reviewed positive training families and 100 reviewed positive development families before counting or scoring a new selection study; never add overlapping fold appearances or choose scope to pass. Require labels knowable before each checkpoint, prior Congress training, reviewed global-family separation, core-only preprocessing and separate earlier calibration. Both classes are necessary, not sufficient. Feature/source missingness must have a frozen policy and a full flow ledger. Cross-block E6 qualification/ranking uses common predeclared core/calibration/evaluation populations, not merely a final common-population refit after incomparable screening.

The maximum original E1–E8 budget is 75 model/period fits with fixed numerical/convergence and calibration procedures. New searches require a prospectively documented amendment, not use of apparently unused slots after seeing scores. Development folds 115–117 and known earlier/later exposures do not become independent because the code changed. A development lead must satisfy the all-fold matched criteria; it is not a promoted model.

Prospective primary evidence uses equal-period Brier, paired rate/stage/temporally admissible survivor comparisons and the registered practical contrast `D_b = mean-period(Brier_model − 0.98 × Brier_b)`. Retain 10,000 paired global-family bootstrap draws, fixed seed and invalid-draw accounting; fewer than 9,000 valid draws blocks interval-based success. The original three practical comparisons use simultaneous 98.3333% two-sided intervals, with upper bounds below zero, favorable period-level Brier and no worse log loss as specified in the protocol. Require the original positive-evaluation-family floors (100 per Congress, 200 unique pooled), integrity and custody gates. These floors are operational, not a power proof; the intervals condition on fitted procedures and observed periods and omit broader future-Congress uncertainty.

## The 60% ambition and controlled learning

Precisely, the goal is `BSS_rate = 1 − Brier_model / Brier_rate ≥ 0.60` on identical eligible cases. Train the historical-rate reference only from eligible earlier data. A 60% rate-baseline improvement does not imply 60% improvement over stage, the survivor, classification accuracy or legal-effect extraction.

Before any protected outcome access, a specific 60% claim requires the additional prespecified contrast `mean-period(Brier_model − 0.40 × Brier_rate)` and a simultaneous interval/multiplicity rule covering it with all confirmatory candidate/comparator claims. Do not substitute a favorable point estimate for interval evidence, alter the baseline or stop sampling when the percentage is reached. Report raw loss, period signs, positive/negative errors and coverage. A negative or inconclusive result is publishable evidence if faithfully reported.

“Self-learning” is a separately registered E8 policy comparison: fixed encoding and update cadence, only matured and audited earlier labels, immutable paired forecasts and comparison with retaining the initial model. Monitoring is not retraining; agent confidence is not a reward or outcome. Protected 120 outcomes cannot be consumed while 121 rules/predictions remain open. The January 2027 freeze boundary, independent custody and earliest 2031 horizon remain controlling; missing readiness requires a later untouched boundary. No software or source-preparation result automatically changes production.

## Additional provenance boundaries and concrete work queue

Bill identity must include Congress, chamber/type, number and text version, with raw-byte and rendered-view hashes. A shared number across Congresses is not the same bill; an introduced and later amended text are not interchangeable. Distinct bill IDs may nevertheless be companions, reintroductions or share provisions. Preserve typed, reviewed family ties for leakage exclusion; an automated graph is only screening evidence. Later-discovered family ties can purge leakage but cannot become historical predictors unless known by the checkpoint.

Calendar inputs must distinguish deterministic constitutional Congress boundaries from session position, announced sitting days and scheduled hearings/markups. An announcement already published at prediction time can be a feature under a registered coverage policy; the event's eventual occurrence, actual adjournment date, cancellation or revised agenda learned later cannot. Preserve each announcement vintage and effective/retrieval times. Missing scraped schedules are unknown, not zero opportunity. Test end-of-session boundary days, timezone normalization, announcements revised after the cutoff and events scheduled after the cutoff but announced before it. Keep deterministic remaining-Congress time separate from realized session length or eventual floor action.

The renderer's complete character coverage does not establish complete source-document coverage. Tables, unrepresented XML cells, unusual wrappers, formulas, footnotes and figures need an explicit raw-XML inventory and render-disposition report. v4 leaf-only matching can intentionally miss text outside represented leaf text nodes even when the legacy scan covers rendered characters. Before further semantic claims, create synthetic table/cell, nested wrapper and mixed-content fixtures; compare raw logical content inventory with the rendering and retain skipped/unsupported-node failures. Do not count an unrepresented cell as a verified absence. The current guard's `source_document_completeness_verified: false` remains appropriate until this independent reconciliation is established.

No current deterministic extractor is a learned model. Agent-authored annotations and adjudications remain provisional modern machine judgments. A future trained extractor must split complete families/documents before learning vocabulary, thresholds or annotation conventions; it needs sufficient independently reviewed positive and negative bills, not a random effect-row split. Pretrained LLMs/embeddings introduce possible later-text/outcome exposure even with source-only prompts. Any such model requires a separate design declaring training provenance/cutoff, exposure limitations, retrieval rules and untouched prospective evaluation; disabling network access does not prove absence of pretrained knowledge.
