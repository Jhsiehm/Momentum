# Next official-context experiment: methods decision draft

November 9, 2026. **Methods proposal updated after the 100-source audit; independent review and a separate execution registration remain required.** No new network requests, outcomes, model fits or predictive scores were accessed to prepare this decision. This is a concrete proposed next study, not a released registration. It neither changes the frozen E3 forecast experiment nor reinterprets its unresolved source/review/positive-family gates.

## Decision and estimand

Proceed toward one small **current-rendition retrospective association diagnostic**, not another text architecture search. Question: does reconstructed pre-day-180 procedural context improve own-bill public-law enactment probabilities over the publication-feature anchor, while also outperforming the stage-rate reference? Enactment must occur after the checkpoint and before Congress end. Historical forecast validity remains blocked by unverified contemporaneous source availability. That limitation does not prevent a separately and explicitly authorized retrospective calculation, but forbids historical-forecast, promotion, self-learning-success or 60%-target-validation claims.

The previous text diagnostic's exception is not inherited. A new protocol must expressly define this diagnostic's interpretation, computations, minimum computational requirements and limitations. Its missing qualified-human certification and original operational positive-family floors remain failed/unknown E3 gates, not “passed” by agent review. Two classes in training are necessary to fit; that fact alone is not adequate event coverage or statistical power.

## Freeze the smallest arms before outcomes

**A — reconstructed publication anchor:** preserve the six existing feature formulas: publication stage/4; capped bill age/730; capped eligible publication-version count/30; capped days since last publication/730; Senate indicator; capped publication count in preceding 30 days/10. The legacy implementation uses publication availability, not general action dates. A current BILLSTATUS edition date is not automatically an availability date. Use exact edition codes IH/IS→0, RH/RS→1, origin-chamber EH/ES→2, ENR→3; opposite-chamber engrossment does not raise the originating-bill stage. Validate the edition from the official package identity and named edition, reject disagreement, deduplicate identical package IDs and reject conflicting duplicate dates. Unknown edition codes do not receive an inferred stage. The unique matching introduced edition may be dated after introduction: require introduction date ≤ introduced-edition date < cutoff, not exact equality; pre-introduction or cutoff/later editions cannot anchor the feature. Require an explicit source-to-field map and name the new input version “retrospective publication-date assertions”; do not claim exact legacy source equivalence. If edition dates/codes cannot reconstruct these features unambiguously, this design is not ready; do not substitute general actions silently.

**B — A plus a fixed procedural context block:** active cosponsor count at cutoff; count of distinct committees referred to by cutoff; days since last qualifying action. Use `log1p` for the two counts, and action recency divided by 180, bounded to [0,1]. Impute missing transformed context values with each feature's observed training median and append three missingness indicators: six anchor columns plus three context columns plus three indicators, twelve total. If any context field has no observed training values, stop the whole diagnostic before fitting. Do not drop it or impute an unsupported constant. Training-only scaling uses population standard deviation with floor .1. A separately versioned dimension-general adapter is required because the frozen structured-scaler API accepts exactly six columns; do not modify the registered six-column implementation. Missingness may encode collection bias and is part of the retrospective limitation. Freeze deduplication, committee-code mapping, valid action codes, withdrawal date treatment and date-only boundary semantics in tested extractor code before any labels. Do not add unrestricted action text, current counts, final stage, later summaries or labels as predictors.

This three-field block is a deliberate **pre-score revision** of the earlier four-field candidate, not a statement that the earlier proposal passed. Sponsor party at introduction is deferred: presence of a sponsor/party field in today's XML does not prove historical affiliation semantics, and a current member party is insufficient. Adding party requires dated affiliation evidence and another prospective design decision, not performance-driven feature selection. If referral histories or cosponsor withdrawal histories cannot be reconstructed, stop this design and report the failed readiness condition; do not choose a favorable subset after scores.

**References:** Beta(1,1) historical rate from the same training rows; the same four-stage smoothed rate, `(stage positives + 5 × training rate)/(stage rows + 5)`. References and both arms predict identical evaluation rows. No text arm, hyperparameter search, probability calibration, model selection or retraining on evaluation cases. Use the already tested penalized logistic objective and optimizer unchanged after a new input-dimension preflight. Two fitted artifacts total, each evaluated in both later periods; this is a fixed-artifact transfer diagnostic, not four separate training fits.

## One bounded source-only population rule

Before further acquisition, register the accepted twelve current GovInfo directory inventories and their hashes. From each of Congresses 113–118, take the first **2,000 identities** under ascending SHA-256 of `official-context-diagnostic-v1\n` plus canonical bill ID, across both chambers together. This gives a fixed maximum of **12,000 candidates**, with no outcome balancing, bill-number interpolation, outcome-dependent replacement or extension until enough positives appear. First exclude the protected 100-case pilot and its known provisional families, and the 595 previously scored evaluation identities and their known families, using frozen existing exposure/family manifests before hashing. These exclusions themselves create an exposed/source-conditioned population, not an untouched confirmatory sample. Retain every exclusion and any later-discovered family collisions.

Train both artifacts on accepted candidates from Congresses 113–116 (at most 8,000); evaluate independently on 117 and 118 (at most 2,000 each). Training outcomes must have matured before the earliest evaluation checkpoint. **Calibration/selection partition: none; zero calibration fits.** This is intentional for the first diagnostic; report raw probability reliability without calling the models calibrated. A later calibrated study needs its own earlier calibration cohort and fixed adequacy requirements.

Acquire at most one current BILLSTATUS XML per selected identity with the already reviewed bounded transport rules; reuse an already captured selected source only by pinning its exact hash and capture time, not by silently mixing updated renditions. Cap each new response at 2,000,000 bytes plus sentinel, no retries/replacements, maximum 12,000 source attempts, 500,000,000 retained response bytes across the batch, at most four concurrent requests and a 30-minute acquisition dispatch window. Reserve the maximum response allowance before dispatch so in-flight requests cannot overshoot the byte ceiling. Schedule the fixed candidate ranks round-robin across Congresses so a cap does not systematically acquire only training periods. In-flight requests finish after dispatch stops; the 20-second socket timeout limits socket inactivity, not total response duration. Thus this is not a strict 30-minute whole-run limit, and subsequent offline feature processing is outside the dispatch window. Preserve requested, failed and not-attempted dispositions; no replacement or cap extension after inspecting outcomes. This is an acquisition ceiling, not an estimate of storage, runtime or cost. Keep failures in the population ledger. The 100-case audit is a readiness study, not a positive-example quota or training sample.

Freeze all source-only inclusion rules and extractor outputs before labels: valid identity/introduction, cutoff strictly before Congress end, computable six-field baseline and representable observed/missing states for each added field, no duplicate identities, known cross-role families removed under a fixed priority of later evaluation over training. Distinguish truly empty histories from absent/unsupported schemas. Require all six anchor fields, but retain context-missing rows identically in both arms under the fixed training-median/indicator policy. Missing is not an observed zero. Report coverage and each missingness pattern against the entire selected candidate set, since source failures and anchor eligibility can bias results.

Only then open a separately implemented outcome-only partition. Use the expressly limited source-proxy rule below; retain unknown or date-ambiguous outcomes as exclusions, never automatic negatives. Report exact class/family counts by period and every exclusion. Freeze counts before fitting. No guarantee of “meaningful” positive counts follows from the 12,000 budget. Both training classes and valid nonempty evaluation periods are computational requirements; zero evaluation events permits only overall losses and explicitly prevents event-discrimination assessment. Sparse events require broad uncertainty and no strong efficacy claim; do not expand after discovering a desired result or event shortage.

## What the 100-source audit can and cannot release

The completed audit retained 100 HTTP 200 responses (1,441,575 bytes); all 100 parsed and passed identity/introduction-date checks, with no reported case errors. Eighty-eight have calendar-only day-180 cutoffs before Congress end; this is not at-risk eligibility. The 100 derived inspection records show 469 action items and 100 sponsor items; action and sponsor containers occur in all 100 cases. Cosponsor containers occur in 82 cases with 984 items; 18 absent containers are unknown, not established zeros. All 100 have text-version containers, with 122 edition metadata records. The schema paths show action codes in 100, dated committee-activity and action committee-identifier paths in 99, sponsor-party paths in 100, and cosponsor withdrawal-date paths in two. These are structural presence counts, not validated field values or an absence-of-withdrawals certification. This methods review inspected only the saved derived allowlists and schema paths, not XML outcome values. Independent audit replay has passed; it verifies software/source reproduction rather than historical availability or field semantics. It does **not** establish sponsor affiliation-at-introduction, action-code semantics, committee-referral reconstruction, complete negative labels, historical source custody or family independence. It cannot by itself release the model run.

Remaining reversible engineering: exact legacy/modern XML adapters; tested publication-edition stage/date mapping; deduplicated action/committee and cosponsor-history extraction; frozen source manifest; same-row family split; a separated outcome resolver; independent pre-fit code/methods review and post-fit prediction/metric replay. These should be one bounded implementation batch using the audit's observed schemas, not another open-ended source investigation.

Irreducible limitations of the current source: retrospectively reported event dates are not contemporaneous capture/publication proof; presently absent historical withdrawals/actions may be unknowable; uncertified negative follow-up/families and previous development exposure cannot be erased. Fail a required semantic reconstruction rather than assigning its unknowns zero. E3 remains blocked regardless of diagnostic results.

## Fixed date, context and source-proxy outcome rules

Treat each source event/edition date as a calendar-day assertion, even when its serialization contains a time. Retain the original precision and timestamp; do not interpret edition midnight/UTC offsets as an independently observed publication time. A pre-cutoff event is usable only when its calendar date is strictly earlier than the day-180 cutoff date. Same-day events are withheld because ordering is unresolved. Validate introduction and Congress dates; the cutoff is introduction plus 180 days. Dates outside the Congress or inconsistent chronology remain errors, not repaired values.

- **Cosponsors:** require identifiable member records and valid sponsorship dates. Count distinct Bioguide IDs joining before cutoff and with no asserted withdrawal on/before cutoff; future withdrawals are not applied to the earlier active set. A join or withdrawal dated exactly on the cutoff date makes the aggregate unknown because within-day ordering is unresolved. A join and withdrawal on the same earlier calendar day is inactive at cutoff, provided the dates and identity agree; conflicting duplicate histories remain unknown. An absent/unsupported container, missing join date or unresolved identity is unknown. Missing withdrawal elements mean only no withdrawal is asserted in this current record, not certified complete history.
- **Referral breadth:** use distinct top-level committee system codes with an activity named exactly `Referred to` and a valid activity date before cutoff. Count referral-ever by cutoff, not currently active referrals; exclude subcommittees from this specified breadth. Unknown activity names are not reclassified by regex. An absent/unsupported committee container or contradictory identifier/date is unknown. Explicitly present, parseable history with no qualifying referral is a reconstructed zero, conditional on the current-record completeness assumption. Do not infer referral from a whole-life committee association alone.
- **Action recency:** use dated top-level bill actions, not amendment actions, final/latest-action summaries or unrestricted text. All valid dated top-level actions qualify as activity; deduplicate repeated source/date/code records. Compute days since the latest qualifying pre-cutoff action. Unsupported/invalid dates or no qualifying action produce unknown; do not assign zero recency.
- **Positive label proxy:** a current own-bill public-law list assertion must agree with a terminal public-law action's law number and dated event. Require the exact public-law action mapping tested before labels are opened, not a learned/fuzzy outcome keyword search. Cutoff-date or earlier enactment is excluded from the at-risk population; a positive requires cutoff date < enactment date < Congress-end date. Conflicting list/action assertions, missing dates and end-date ambiguity are unknown.
- **Negative label proxy:** require successfully parsed full current XML with matching identity, valid introduction, supported top-level action history and an explicit record update date strictly after Congress end; neither law entries nor actions may assert public-law enactment. Then label zero **as a current-official-record negative proxy**, not a certified true negative. Otherwise the label is unknown. Retrieval today alone does not replace the required update assertion. No rule relaxation after class counts.

The scientific target remains own-bill enactment, but these observed labels are source proxies: absent omitted enactments, incomplete historical actions or updates unrelated to completeness can still mislabel cases. No independent final-law census is implied. Report positive, negative-proxy and unknown denominators and update-date exclusions separately. Training-label source capture today is an exposed retrospective reconstruction, not proof labels were available for a historical deployment.

Use existing frozen protected-pilot and exposed-evaluation exclusions before candidate ranking; rebuild the available related-bill graph under a fixed union-find rule and retain existing known family links. Current related-bill edges serve only conservative partition protection, not predictors. Preserve all members and graph evidence, remove training families touching either evaluation period, and assign cross-evaluation families to the later period before selecting a deterministic within-family representative. Report that the graph can miss unrecorded companions/reintroductions and cannot certify semantic independence. The available automated screen is sufficient only for this expressly limited diagnostic, not the original human family-review gate.

## Reporting and stopping

Freeze the protocol, inputs, exact accepted identities, labels and solver code before two fits. Save probabilities before scoring. Report each period and equal-period mean: Brier loss (mean squared probability error), log loss, expected versus observed events, class-specific Brier denominators, ten fixed reliability bins, and paired Brier differences against A and both references. Use the existing fixed family-bootstrap method only with explicitly provisional-family, selected-data and exposed-period limitations; do not treat two Congresses as a well-estimated population of political environments. Record failures/nonconvergence without retries. No score-dependent refits or promotion.

The immediate action is an independent go/no-go on reconstructing the six baseline fields and three context fields, followed by the separately frozen acquisition/extractor/label protocol above. If the source cannot supply that question, publish the specific failed field mapping and stop this proposed study; do not continue collecting unrelated tables in hope of a better score.
