# Forecasting-first execution plan v1

November 12, 2026 (America/New_York). Authorized work: implement and independently challenge a separate passage study, learn from documented failures, and prioritize forecasting evidence over unrelated product expansion. This plan is not a claim of predictive success or permission to reinterpret earlier studies.

## Objective and scope

Estimate whether an at-risk H.R. or S. bill first passes its originating chamber during the 90 days following a fixed day-30 checkpoint, using information actually available by that checkpoint. Determine whether a structured challenger adds useful probability accuracy over a strong stage/chamber reference on the same cases. Preserve the day-180 enactment paper and the existing day-30/by-Congress-end passage benchmark as separate targets.

Software completion, source feasibility, retrospective development, and independent prospective validation are separate milestones. A blocked source gate or a baseline that wins is a valid result. No new real fitting, scoring, or live issuance is authorized by a successful unit test alone.

## What previous failures change

- **Outcome-associated selection:** the publication gate excluded all 333 current-law assertion cases in the 12,000-source study. Inventory candidates before filtering; retain per-case dispositions and report coverage alongside scores. Inspect selection by outcomes only in the explicitly exposed feasibility/development population, never to tune protected evaluation membership. [Receipt](../operations/runs/20261109-official-context-diagnostic.md).
- **Parser success on fixtures was insufficient:** unsupported legitimate action encodings made all procedural fields missing; a signing annotation then rejected otherwise corroborated outcomes. Keep exact source identity and narrow semantics; test actual captured source shapes, invalid shapes and positive controls. Save failures before registering amendments. [Resolution](../operations/runs/20261111-action-context-resolution.md).
- **Missing is not negative:** passage research had hundreds of unresolved outcomes despite accessible action lists. A negative needs reviewed complete coverage through its own horizon; absence of a recognized code is only unresolved. [Negative audit](../operations/runs/20261105-negative-passage-audit.md).
- **Printed dates are not availability:** modern renditions and empty archive searches did not establish historic bytes. Capture exact bytes now with actual acquisition times. Distinguish local observation from independently attested possession and historical availability. [Archive feasibility](../operations/runs/20261108-historical-next-feasibility.md).
- **Weak comparators exaggerated the apparent opportunity:** November 11 context gain was 3.04% versus the small structured baseline, but 0.83% versus the stronger milestone reference, with uncertainty including zero. Use the stronger reference as primary, plus a global rate diagnostic; never substitute the larger headline after results.
- **Exposed periods remain exposed:** parser repairs, source inspection and earlier fits consumed historical independence. Congresses 113–118 can support clearly labeled development, not a new untouched test. Family links and repeated observations also invalidate naive independent-row uncertainty.
- **Passing tests did not establish forecasting value:** preserve the distinction among arithmetic replay, runtime fault testing, agent review, qualified evidence review and independent predictive validation. No agent can sign an uncompleted human/custodian gate.

## Ordered delivery stages

### 1. Freeze the question and observation schedule

Deliver `PASSAGE90_PROTOCOL_V1.md` and a separate versioned contract module. One checkpoint per bill at introduction plus exactly 30 days; horizon 90 days after that checkpoint. Count first originating-chamber passage of that bill, not other-chamber action, repassage after earlier success, enactment, or incorporation into another vehicle. Define date-only/boundary handling, identity rules and the risk set explicitly.

Require the full horizon strictly before Congress end. Retain late introductions in the inventory with an exclusion reason; do not carry their identity into a new Congress or shorten their horizon silently. On November 12, 2026 a new day-30 checkpoint cannot have a complete 90-day window within the current Congress. Work now can prepare and collect evidence; eligible future issuance needs a separately frozen future schedule.

Exit: valid and invalid target/time cases independently checked; exact version and code hashes recorded. No claim that passage supplies more usable positive cases until measured.

### 2. Measure source and label feasibility without fitting

Implement a bounded current-source pilot with two Congress-118 GovInfo inventory requests and ten deterministic hash-selected identities per chamber (20 bills, maximum 22 requests total). Freeze the inventory rule, salt, timeout, response limits, zero retries/replacements and parser version before requesting bill XML. Retain the exact discovery bytes, every selected identity, every failure and all raw hashes in ignored `data/`.

This small pilot tests the new collector and passage source representation. It is not a representative historical validation cohort, power study, independent complete census, or training set. Current-record passage events are candidate evidence only; missing recognized passage stays unresolved and cannot become a certified negative. No availability is backdated. Report full-window and pre-checkpoint-event dispositions before discussing usable outcomes.

Exit: independently replayable per-case inventory, source and passage-feasibility receipt, including negative/blocked results. Any parser amendment after real-source failure gets a new version/run; no silent replacement. Larger acquisition requires the initial receipt to justify the scope and a new fixed manifest.

### 3. Implement the source and paired-forecast ledger

Store exact response bytes, URL, internally generated request and completion times, status, size, digest, discovery/registration binding and failure disposition. Validate pre-existing content-addressed bytes and an append-only receipt chain. Serialize writers and reject backward clocks, tampering and overwrite. Interrupted acquisitions remain visible. An independent verifier recomputes bindings rather than trusting receipt flags.

Implement paired local issuance with the same bill, target, checkpoint and input basis, frozen model/baseline artifacts, valid finite probabilities, source availability by cutoff and a fixed issuance deadline. It must neither fit models nor call the legacy arbitrary-age live model. Local receipts explicitly remain uncertified. Certified prospective issuance stays blocked until a real external timestamp verifier, reviewed model/source artifacts and the required operational/custody controls exist. A user-supplied Boolean or digest is not independent certification.

Exit: positive and negative API/CLI cases, concurrent writes, interrupted writes, clock rollback and tampering tested. Do not invent forecasts just to populate a ledger.

### 4. Freeze comparisons and reporting before predictive evaluation

Primary comparison: structured challenger against training-only stage/chamber/time-remaining historical rates with fixed shrinkage/fallback. Global rate is secondary. Stage categories describe only pre-passage state; time-remaining categories are fixed before results, with sparse cells shrinking to stage/chamber and global rates. Use one deterministic family representative for training, chronological training availability and global family separation; keep evaluation families as clusters. Every method must predict exactly the same cases, or the paired comparison blocks.

Primary practical contrast is mean Brier(challenger) minus 0.98 times mean Brier(reference), i.e. at least 2% relative loss reduction. The registered uncertainty criterion must address that practical contrast, not only an interval for any nonzero gain. Show each congressional period and both aggregate weighting rules; family-level resampling cannot establish broad period-level generalization from very few Congresses.

Secondary outputs: log loss, reliability bins with counts, expected/observed events, positive/negative-specific loss, fixed top-20 prioritization precision and event capture, coverage and missingness. Fixed-checkpoint top-20 results are a cohort diagnostic, not a claim about a weekly monitoring product. Reject non-finite/out-of-range probabilities, duplicate identities, unresolved outcomes and mismatched method populations.

Exit: synthetic numerical/adversarial verification and frozen configuration. Real development fitting still requires eligible training cases and reviewed labels; this fresh checkout has no prior ignored runtime corpus. The initial source pilot does not waive that requirement. Record unavailable prerequisites precisely rather than refitting on an arbitrary convenient sample.

### 5. Develop and freeze a candidate only after evidence gates

Use exposed chronological periods for development; choose finite fit/calibration budgets, candidate features, hyperparameters, family rules and success criteria before scores. Compare simple structured inputs first. Add bill text only through a later matched structured-versus-structured-plus-text registration, with frozen preprocessing and evaluated extraction where applicable. Preserve prior negative results.

Exit: a reproducible artifact containing exact training/label/source memberships, target/version, transformations, coefficients and baseline counts, independently replayed. The new study cannot reuse an enactment artifact merely because feature dimensions match. If adequate historical training is unavailable, collect a development cohort forward and wait for its labels, then freeze for a later evaluation cohort.

### 6. Launch a genuinely future, fixed study

Proposed earliest evaluation frame: H.R./S. bills introduced January 3–June 30, 2027 (Congress 120), each at its day-30 checkpoint, with a fixed November 15, 2027 analysis date after the final 90-day horizons mature. Exact UTC/date-only protocol boundaries apply. This single-Congress pilot cannot establish cross-Congress consistency. It is a proposed frame, not an active registered launch. Before the cohort starts, freeze all artifacts, actual availability/attestation route, census discovery rule/cadence, issuance tolerance, minimum evidence requirements, missingness sensitivity, outcome adjudication and access/custodian arrangement. If readiness misses that boundary, register a later untouched frame before its cohort starts; no backdating.

Every eligible missed/late/failed issuance remains in the coverage denominator. Record paired forecasts atomically and immutably, monitor operational completeness without using evaluation losses to select versions, and reconcile final inventory and family links. New model versions get separately registered cohorts. Local observation-only captures can begin before independent certification, with their limitation visible.

Exit: completed issuance coverage and matured, reviewed outcomes. A 90-day horizon creates earlier resolving cases but no guarantee of sufficient events or useful power. Prespecify a power/precision assessment on development data rather than equating arbitrary event floors with power. No extend-until-success stopping.

### 7. Report, challenge and decide

Publish calibration, paired losses, prioritization, coverage, every period, uncertainty and reviewed error cases. Use distinct outcomes: supported practical gain, evidence of harm/no useful gain, inconclusive, or blocked/invalid evidence. No result automatically promotes a model. Preserve per-case manifests, immutable hashes, protocols, code and exact reproduction commands; raw source/runtime data remain ignored.

## Independent audit and work ownership

The primary chat integrates the plan, bounded pilot, documentation and release checks. Separate subagents implement the passage contract and ledger; a third independently reviews methods and adversarial cases. The user-requested audit chat, **Momentum — Independent failure-path audit**, audits reachable existing behavior in its own checkout and reviews new code without authoring the implementation it certifies.

Audit expectations extend beyond offline unit tests: real local CLI/HTTP/persistence paths; malformed and missing input; future evidence/labels; wrong identity/chamber; incomplete negative coverage; family contamination; one-class training; non-finite probabilities; duplicate/late issuance; corrupted hashes; network failure, partial bodies and interrupted/concurrent writes. Valid positive controls must still pass. For selected safeguards, deliberately disable the guard in a disposable copy and verify that the test fails. Report audit scope and gaps rather than claiming that all possible failures were tested.

Each meaningful batch updates the development log and relevant changelog, records actual validation, reviews the diff, commits and pushes to the Momentum repository. Research-affecting defects must be reproduced, fixed narrowly, and independently retested without rewriting earlier artifacts. Audit findings and implementation status belong in execution receipts, not retrospective edits to this plan's evidence claims.

## Completion limits

This batch can deliver the plan, separate target contract, guarded source/forecast ledger, comparison machinery, bounded source feasibility and independent runtime audit. It cannot produce future outcomes, qualified human reviews, external timestamp certification, or an eligible trained model by declaration. Every later stage remains explicitly pending until its evidence exists. The next achievement is a trustworthy evaluation cycle; demonstrated predictive value remains an empirical question.
