# Experiment validity: independent methods audit

November 8, 2026. Reviewer: rubric_v2_methods. Scope: frozen protocols, exposed-design references, source coverage, candidate expansion, context triage, evaluator and runners. No protected quality text, individual outcomes, new predictive studies or network requests were accessed. Public prior aggregate readiness summaries were read; this audit is not wholly outcome-naive. The reviewer previously implemented or replayed several components, so this is a cross-check within the agent team, not external validation.

**Conclusion:** the recent experiments support reproducible software and source-location findings. They do not establish semantic extraction quality, historically eligible predictors, learned generalization or predictive improvement. Repeated use of design data is legitimate development when disclosed; relabeling its gains as independent accuracy would be invalid. The existing gates remain blocked or unestablished. No reason was found to discard correctly sealed raw artifacts or rerun predictive experiments through a bypass.

## Concrete next-stage plan and exit gates

1. **Freeze the audit and exposure inventory.** Bind every prior protocol, rule/reference version, sample and result; record which design feedback motivated each amendment. Preserve failed runs and M12 as a deviation. Success: a replayable inventory with distinct exploratory and protected populations. Failure: unknown exposure or missing evidence blocks any independent claim; it does not justify selecting another convenient test subset.

2. **Repair engineering defects in new versions only.** Add the M05 no-cost/coordinated-clause contrasts alongside varied-subject positive examples. Harden a new execution guard for exact bill populations, all twelve statuses where required, immutable input/code binding, known flags, duplicate rejection and source evidence. Run adversarial tests normally and with optimized Python. Success: explicit checks reject tampering and preserve all original artifacts. Failure: stop the new run; do not patch registered old code or declare prior failures erased. A software pass remains an engineering pass.

3. **Register one bounded design rerun.** Before opening its results, seal rules, reference hash, full forty-case population, budgets, diagnostic definitions and sample selection. Compare old/new outputs against identical reference quotations; report retained/recovered/lost identities, workload and misleading contexts. Include bill- and mechanism-level summaries so long bills do not dominate the only result. Freeze any failure or tradeoff. Improved locator coverage can nominate another development version; it cannot release a semantic or predictive gate. Avoid repeated search until a desired percentage appears.

4. **Resolve reference and source readiness independently.** Set compound-effect granularity and review examples on exposed design data. Do not silently pool the earlier ten v1-design annotations with v2/v3. Complete genuine reviewer qualifications, source/family dispositions and historical bindings. If historical bytes remain unverifiable, a separate present-day annotation study may still be useful, but historical prediction remains blocked. Modern source-local rules do not make modern source bytes historically available.

5. **Run E0 quality only after its actual gates pass.** Freeze the exact original 50 quality identities, full-view renderer, extractor, abstention policy, matching instructions and failures before access. Two qualified humans independently annotate full documents without candidate output or each other's annotations; a third adjudicates, retaining uncertainty. Require 100% resolved evidence references, zero unresolved identity/future-availability errors, and at least 20 positive quality bills per field before applying the existing ≥95% precision/≥90% recall engineering thresholds. Record sparse fields as uncertified; do not enlarge the sample after inspecting labels. For detailed effects, settle the draft's additional joint-semantic gate prospectively. Use one-to-one compatibility matching, explicit uncertain-reference exclusions, predicted-abstention penalties on positive statuses, micro and equal-bill macro reporting, and bill/family dependence-aware uncertainty. Failure stays frozen; further tuning consumes this quality sample and requires a separately registered fresh one.

6. **Release predictive blocks only through their separate prerequisites.** A methods reviewer must name the exact population to which 200 reviewed positive training-family and 100 reviewed positive development-family floors apply; scope must not be chosen because it passes. Require temporal label availability, independently reviewed families, eligible sources, core-only preprocessing, disjoint calibration and both classes. Sparse calibration remains a disclosed risk even after a computational class check. E1 may use its existing-source path; richer E2–E5 cannot inherit readiness from XML counts. Preserve the fixed solver/convergence and 75-fit ceiling. Match every control/treatment population; use the common-population clarification for E6 qualification and selection. All 115–117 results remain exposed development. A blocked required fold blocks the all-fold decision; reversed signs or failed loss criteria are not an all-fold lead.

7. **Separate prospective prediction from controlled updating.** Preserve the existing survivor and challenger. A richer candidate is a successor, not a replacement inside the frozen protocol. Freeze protected analysis/capture/custody before the intended future whole-Congress boundary; if the January 2027 deadline is missed, use a later untouched boundary without backdating. Do not update from protected 120 outcomes into open 121 predictions. E8 updating requires its own fixed policy, matured labels and retained-model comparator; more documents or more agent reviews are not self-learning evidence.

8. **Report success, failure and uncertainty using the registered target.** The estimand is own-bill public-law enactment conditional on being unenacted at day 180. Report same-case Brier and log loss, rate/stage/temporally admissible survivor comparisons, equal-period aggregate losses, coverage and event errors. Preserve the paper's paired global-family bootstrap, invalid-draw rules, simultaneous practical-effect criteria and event floors; these condition on fitted artifacts and observed periods, not future-Congress generalization. A specific 60% claim additionally needs a prespecified `Brier_model − 0.40 × Brier_rate` contrast and multiplicity/interval rule. Missing provenance is **blocked**; evidence against the registered practical gain is **negative**; sparse or crossing intervals and mixed periods are **inconclusive**. None may be relabeled as success because the workflow ran.
