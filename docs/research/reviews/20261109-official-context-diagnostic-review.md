# Independent official context diagnostic review

November 9, 2026. This review covers separately registered current-source acquisition, feature preparation, and a proposed two-fit retrospective context diagnostic. It does not release E3, certify historical source availability, validate true-negative labels or approve promotion.

## Acquisition and feature preflight

The source-only acquisition received an explicit independent pass before execution. **32 tests passed normally and with optimized Python**: 18 author tests and 14 independent tests. Reviewed fixed per-Congress hash selection, protected/exposed identity and known-family exclusions, round-robin scheduling, four-worker maximum, response allowance reservation under a 500 MB aggregate body ceiling, no retries/replacements, and actual saved receipt hashes. The 30-minute limit stops new scheduling; pending requests and offline feature processing are outside that scheduling limit. Known-family coverage is limited to the frozen available manifests, not a complete legislative genealogy.

Preflight corrected several substantive risks before registration: permissive edition URLs could accept an unrelated host or filename; out-of-Congress introduction dates could pass identity checks; unsupported/duplicate XML containers could hide unparsed history while yielding zero counts; invalid timestamp hours could be truncated into a valid date; and a signing-only action with a later record update could incorrectly become a negative proxy. Independent tests reproduced the signing-only error, then verified that it becomes unknown. Initial independent fixture errors used an invalid dummy Bioguide identifier; the fixtures were corrected before successful testing, without inspecting real outcomes.

The frozen mapping uses exact GovInfo package/rendition identity and edition type, explicit dates, supported container shapes and conservative unknowns. Ordinary known-source actions may legitimately lack action codes. Enactment requires a supported code/source pairing and anchored terminal public-law assertion corroborated by the own-bill law list. Signing-only, private-law and inconsistent enactment assertions remain unknown. Date-only events at the checkpoint are not assumed earlier. Cosponsor withdrawal after cutoff does not change earlier count; cutoff-day joins/withdrawals remain unknown. An earlier same-day join and withdrawal is inactive. Current-record missingness and incomplete withdrawal histories remain material limitations.

All original six anchor formulas are retained under newly named retrospective edition-date assertions. A delayed introduced-edition printing may occur between introduction and cutoff; edition dates are not historical availability dates. The three context features plus three missing indicators form twelve columns. This policy was resolved before labels or scores, not selected based on results.

## Fit-runner preflight

**18 tests passed normally and with optimized Python**: ten author tests and eight independent tests. No actual optimization was performed in these runner tests; solver calls in orchestration tests were mocked. The independent tests recomputed adapter medians, population means/standard deviations and missing indicators, and verified held-out transformations leave training state unchanged. Entirely missing training context stops the comparison.

Preflight required and verified three runner corrections: exact feature-file membership must match the complete acquisition ledger; label exclusions must be saved before class/role/maturity gates can fail; and returned model parameters must independently satisfy the frozen objective's finite gradient tolerance, not merely report an accepted flag. An independent adversarial run supplied a fake accepted optimizer result with a nonzero gradient and confirmed rejection after one attempt, with no probabilities produced.

Family tests verify transitive links through external nodes, protected known-family bridges, later-period priority including later feature-ineligible cases, and deterministic within-family representatives. Unknown labels are not replaced, Boolean labels are rejected, and training target-maturity dates must precede the earliest evaluation checkpoint. The feature/family population is saved before invoking the outcome resolver; label counts and exact matrices precede the two fixed fits; predictions are saved before scoring.

This is conditional permission for the explicitly limited source-proxy diagnostic after acquisition integrity review and execution registration. Post-Congress record updates plus no asserted public law are **uncertified negative proxies**, not evidence of complete negative follow-up. Current related-bill graphs and prior exposure do not become certified independence or untouched validation. No score-dependent refitting, calibration search or promotion is permitted.

Neither a redownload nor relaxation of the all-missing gate is justified. The original registered v1 inputs, implementation, outputs and negative result remain immutable.
