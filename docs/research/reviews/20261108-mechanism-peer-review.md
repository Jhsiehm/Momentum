## Unresolved boundaries and follow-up challenges

1. **Authorization consistency.** Preserving Medicaid waiver permission is present, while permission within replacement-payee duties and tax-favored transactions remains uncertain. These distinctions are plausible but not fully specified in the registration. A future rubric must say whether authorization requires a new discretion, includes preservation of existing discretion, or includes tax eligibility. Do not derive a universal rule from these three cases after observing labels.
2. **Prohibition versus exclusion.** The payee applicability exception and tax deduction exclusion remain uncertain; denial of particular hiring powers is present. The latter has explicit scoped operative wording, making the present decision defensible. Future instructions still need an explicit boundary for restrictions on legal effects versus restrictions on conduct.
3. **Designation, repeal, sunset and penalties.** The retained uncertain decisions accurately expose missing category boundaries. State explicitly whether designation includes appointments, naming a newly created fund, or authorization of an honor; whether repeal includes replacement and abrogation; whether finite funding authority is a sunset; and whether sanction exposure counts as an affirmative requirement. No forced consensus is warranted here.
4. **Certification timing.** The opioid bill has no separate quantified filing deadline, supporting absent under the current interpretation. A future definition of deadline should explicitly address event-triggered prerequisites such as certification with each registration, so the label is not confused with the prescription-duration limit.
5. **Historical legal change versus visible text.** Introduced amendment text can establish a visible operation without establishing all changes relative to prior law. Retrieval and review of referenced/displaced provisions would be a separately documented evidence extension; absence of those sources should remain visible, especially for repeal labels.
