# Legislative forecasting workspace
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Implementation assumption: Python standard library service and browser-native JavaScript; local-first research prototype.
## Users
Policy researchers and lobbyists inspecting federal bill movement and dated evidence, as specified in the supplied plan.
## Product Purpose
Reconstruct the evidence available at a cutoff, save a forecast, inspect earlier reference classes, then reveal and score outcomes.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Enactment by Congress end is the first target. Synthetic demonstration and official-source records are separate datasets. No historical calibration or 50-year completeness claim. Product name remains undecided.
## Brand Commitments
Supplied plan: restrained olive and graphite, warm reading surfaces, etched geometry, prominent functional bill landscape, accessible flat and table alternatives.
## Evidence on Hand
Revision 2 implementation plan. Empty initial repository. GovInfo public XML supports import, but historical availability must be audited.
## Product Principles
Dated evidence precedes inference. Unknown is not zero. Locked forecasts survive outcome reveal. AI extraction is separately evaluated.
